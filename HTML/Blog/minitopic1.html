Positive Impacts of Technology on Watches

Electronic technology has pervaded the watch industry in many ways and has offered benefits that positively improve form and function. Perhaps one of the biggest shifts as a result of smartwatches is changing the face of the timepiece or watch. Unlike simple applications that provide entertainment, smartwatches have such additional applications as health, GPS navigation, notifications about different events, and fitness to make healthy and convenient watches simultaneously. Smartwatches, for example, popularised by Apple, Samsung, and Garmin are no longer just devices that track time.

Also, the boosting concept of sustainable technologies has brought new environmentally friendly ideas to the creation of watches. An instance is solar watches which are powered by sunlight but require little or no battery power thereby easing the cycle of having to replace batteries and hence avoiding electronic pollution. Manufacturers like Seiko and Citizen have adopted this technology knowing it will match the increasing environmental concern.

Nanotechnology has also been a breakthrough in developing technology-enhanced 3D printing for prototyping and custom creations. This allows manufacturers to develop complicated designs and non-trivial combinations of materials that offer the opportunity to release limited or personalized models of watches. It accomplishes this by increasing the speed of manufacturing thus making it more efficient and minimizing wastage of materials needed in production.

In addition, marketing and selling have been made easier through the help of technology. Buying-and-selling, virtual try-ons with the usage of AR, and machine learning suggest outfits increase brand visibility and create unique customer experiences. These are the innovations that not only rev up sales volumes but also increase customer satisfaction.

Therefore, technology has shaken the watch industry in a positive way, wherein the combination of vintage artisan work mixed with new technology leads to more practical, environmentally friendly, and appealing timepieces for different demographics.
